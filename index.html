About Alora

Alora C. Kane writes alpha king and werewolf romance. Fated mates, rejected omegas, secret heirs, and proud alpha males brought to their knees by their sassy, often omega, mates. These are romances, always, with the happy ending that is earned.

The tales unfold in old-world kingdoms and in the modern day, where the old laws still hold. Every story is original, richly illustrated, and narrated like a fireside tale. Most run over an hour.

Midnight Crown Stories is one author, not a studio. I write every story myself, then use AI tools to help edit, illustrate, and give each tale its voice. Care takes time, so uploads are rare and worth the wait.
